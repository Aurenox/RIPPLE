RIPPLE OPERATIONS KNOWLEDGE BASE
Version: 3.2

ORDER PROCESSING POLICY

1. Standard orders below ₹50,000 should be verified and processed within 24 hours.

2. Orders above ₹50,000 require manager approval before processing.

3. Orders marked as high-risk require additional verification by the Operations Compliance Team.

4. The Operations Team is responsible for order verification, customer information validation, and status updates.

5. If an order is delayed for more than 24 hours, the Operations Team must notify the responsible manager.

6. Express orders have a target processing time of 8 hours and should be prioritized over standard orders.

7. Manager approval is required when changing the processing rules for high-value orders.


CUSTOMER SUPPORT PROCEDURE

1. Customer complaints related to delayed orders should first be assigned to the Customer Support Team.

2. If the delay is caused by order verification, Customer Support should coordinate with Operations.

3. Complaints involving high-value orders should be escalated to a manager.

4. Repeated complaints involving the same process should be reviewed for a possible operational issue.


OPERATIONS WORKFLOW

1. Order received
   ↓
2. Customer information validation
   ↓
3. Order verification
   ↓
4. Risk assessment
   ↓
5. Manager approval if required
   ↓
6. Order processing
   ↓
7. Customer notification

8. Removing the verification step may reduce processing time, but could increase the risk of incorrect or fraudulent orders.

9. Automating standard-order approval may reduce workload for managers, but the system must continue to route high-value and high-risk orders for human review.


ESCALATION POLICY

1. Processing delays above 24 hours are considered operational exceptions.

2. Processing delays above 48 hours require manager review.

3. More than 10 similar complaints within one week should trigger an operational investigation.

4. Any change affecting approval rules must be reviewed before implementation.


KNOWN OPERATIONAL ISSUE

Recent reports indicate that order-processing delays have increased.

Several delayed orders were waiting for verification or manager approval.

The Operations Team has reported increased workload during periods of high order volume.

Customer Support has also reported an increase in complaints related to delayed deliveries.


PROPOSED CHANGE

A possible improvement is to introduce automated approval for standard orders below ₹50,000.

High-value orders and high-risk orders should continue to require human approval.

Before implementing the change, its effects on processing time, operational workload, compliance risk, and customer complaints should be evaluated.
